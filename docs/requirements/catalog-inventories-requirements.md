# Catalog and inventory requirements

## US-03 Product catalog and commercial management

Epic E02. A store administrator can manage the store offering, including cash and credit prices, images and installment eligibility.

- A registered product appears in its store catalog with its prices, unit and available stock.
- Discontinuing a product removes it from checkout. The persisted record and historical references remain available.

## US-04 Inventory monitoring and low-stock alerts

Epic E02. A store administrator can view stock and identify items that need replenishment.

- Confirming a cash or credit sale reduces product stock in the transaction that records the sale.
- The catalog shows current stock and its minimum threshold. Use the product edit form to update stock.
